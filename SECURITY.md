# Security Policy

PhiNode Labs researches and discloses vulnerabilities under coordinated disclosure.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email **research@phinode.io**. Please encrypt sensitive reports with our PGP key.

- Public key: https://keys.openpgp.org/search?q=research@phinode.io
- PGP fingerprint: `C3ED DFC4 48D6 B1EA 42C1 4F88 F1F7 CDFC B2E4 AE75`

Include the affected component and version, the impact, and reproduction steps or a proof of concept.

## Our commitment
We acknowledge reports promptly, coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline with you, credit reporters, and never publish operational exploit detail before a fix is public.
